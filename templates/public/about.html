ABOUT GEOMARK

Revolutionizing Workforce Presence Management

GeoMark is an enterprise Software-as-a-Service platform engineered by Rudvay Technology Solutions.

Our Mission

At GeoMark, we believe that modern organisations deserve transparent, effortless, and tamper-proof attendance management. Traditional biometric fingerprint hardware and manual registers suffer from high failure rates, maintenance costs, and proxy attendance risks.

We solved this by leveraging ubiquitous smartphone technologies—satellite GPS sensors and browser-based WebRTC cameras—to create a zero-hardware presence verification ecosystem.
